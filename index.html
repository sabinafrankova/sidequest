Terms and conditions

Who we are

Sidequest connects people who want to try a hobby with local students and hobbyists who teach it in Montréal. It's run by the five founders of Group 6, who also teach the first classes.

Booking a class

  • A booking holds your spot at one class time.
  • Your first class is free, once per email address. After that, you pay per class. There are no semester fees.
  • Same time every week keeps the same spot for the weeks you choose. You still pay per class and can skip or cancel any week.
  • Bring a friend books a second spot under your friend's own email. If it's their first class, it's free for them too.
  • Please arrive on time and bring what the class lists under "Loot to bring".

Cancelling

You can cancel for free up to 24 hours before class. The full rules, including refunds, are in our Refund policy.

Teachers

Teachers are independent students and hobbyists. They decide what the class covers, its level, times and price. Sidequest checks every new class before it goes live and can remove classes that break these terms.

Taking part safely

Some classes are physical. Tell your teacher about any injury or health issue that matters for the class, and follow their instructions. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under Quebec consumer protection law.

Reviews

Only people who booked a class can review it. Keep reviews honest and respectful. We can remove reviews that are abusive, fake, or share someone's personal details.

Your account

In this demo, logging in needs no password and everything is saved in your own browser. See our Privacy policy.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will change the date at the top when we do. These terms follow the laws of Quebec and Canada. Questions? Email the team at [team email].

Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Your name, email, and optionally your phone number and a note for your teacher, when you book.
  • Your friend's first name and email, when you bring a friend.
  • Your rating and review, when you write one.
  • Your email, when you ask for a reminder.
  • The classes you save with the heart.
  • Teachers' name, email, a line about them, class details and an optional photo.

Why we use it

To hold your spot, send your confirmation and reminders, show reviews, and list classes. We also count how people use the site (for example how many bookings get finished) to make it better.

Where it goes

  • Your browser. In this demo, everything is saved only on your own device, not on our servers.
  • EmailJS. When emails are switched on, the email address and booking details needed for the message pass through EmailJS, the service that sends them.
  • Google. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address. "Add to Google Calendar" opens Google Calendar only when you tap it. Google Analytics counts page visits and bookings, but only if you choose "Accept analytics" in the cookie banner.

We never sell your information, and learners never see a teacher's email.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • Necessary (always on). Your bookings, saved classes, log-in and cookie choice are kept in your browser's local storage so the site works. In this demo they never leave your device.
  • Analytics (only with your OK). Google Analytics sets cookies whose names start with _ga to tell visits apart. They stay off until you choose "Accept analytics", and saying no doesn't change anything else on the site.

You can change your choice any time with , which is also at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

Quebec's privacy law (Law 25) and Canadian law give you the right to see, correct and delete the personal information we hold about you. In this demo you can delete everything saved on this device right now.

Refund policy

First class

Your first class is free, so there's nothing to refund. Cancel it anytime so someone else can take the spot.

Paid classes

  • Cancel 24 hours or more before class: full refund.
  • Cancel less than 24 hours before, or don't show up: no refund, because the teacher has already saved your spot.
  • The teacher cancels or the class can't happen: full refund, or move to another time if you prefer.

Same time every week

Each week counts as its own class. Cancel any week 24 hours or more before it for a full refund of that week. Cancelling one week doesn't cancel the others.

Bring a friend

Your friend's spot follows the same rules. Cancelling your booking also cancels your friend's spot.

Review discount

Each first review of a class gives 10% off your next paid class. It has no cash value and can't be refunded.

How to cancel

Go to My bookings and tap "Cancel booking". Refunds go back to the way you paid, usually within 5 to 10 business days.
